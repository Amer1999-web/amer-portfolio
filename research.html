Research

Bridging first‑principles theory, Monte Carlo simulations, and artificial intelligence to design next‑generation energy and functional materials.

Hydrogen Storage Materials

Density Functional Theory (DFT) and thermodynamic modelling of perovskite, double‑perovskite and high‑entropy hydrides. Emphasis on gravimetric capacity, thermodynamic stability, and mechanical properties.

Machine Learning & Quantum ML

Predicting band gaps, lattice parameters, and elastic constants using random forests, support vector machines, deep neural networks, and quantum‑enhanced models (QSVR). Interpretability via SHAP and feature engineering.

Monte Carlo & Spin Dynamics

Atomistic spin simulations and micromagnetic modelling (MuMax3, UppASD) for full‑Heusler alloys, domain wall dynamics, and Curie temperature prediction. Integration with DFT for exchange couplings.

Spintronics & Topological Materials

Electronic structure and spin‑dependent transport in half‑metallic Heusler alloys, topological insulators, and 2D heterostructures. Exploration of Majorana fermions for quantum computing.

Solid‑State Batteries & 2D Materials

First‑principles study of Li‑ion diffusion in MXenes and novel electrode materials. Design of solid electrolytes and analysis of 2D material heterostructures for energy storage.

Method Development

Python‑based micromagnetic finite‑difference codes, high‑throughput DFT workflows, and integration of OpenMP/MPI for large‑scale materials simulations.
