Hello, I’m

Amer Alzoubi

Computational Materials Scientist · Physicist · AI‑Driven Researcher

I discover and design advanced functional materials at the intersection of first‑principles theory and artificial intelligence. My research centers on solid‑state hydrogen storage, perovskite and double‑perovskite hydrides, magnetic systems, and data‑driven materials prediction. By integrating Density Functional Theory, Monte Carlo simulations, spin‑based modeling, and machine learning, I aim to accelerate the emergence of next‑generation energy and functional materials.
